# Deep Research Rules (Always On — Universal v4.0 Standard)

For ANY research task in ANY sphere (finance, law, tax, medicine, science, software, or due diligence) follow `.agents/skills/deep-research/SKILL.md` automatically and by default. Non-negotiable:

1. **Snippets are leads, never evidence.** Facts enter only via `evidence.py fetch` (SHA-256 snapshot) → `evidence.py add --quote "<verbatim>"`. Fix misses at the source — never weaken a claim or use prose labels to dodge missing primary evidence.
2. **Strict Quote & `[C###]` Calculation Binding (gates E8, R2, R3, R9).** Every claim-bearing sentence or table row (including Row 1 of every table) cites `[E###]` (and `[C###]` from `calc.py` for derived math). `Inference:` / `Извод:` is strictly qualitative and **cannot introduce unbound numbers**. Inline `Calculation:` requires an explicit formula/operator and cannot exceed 40% of claim units (gate R9).
3. **Pre-search Kill Hypotheses & Anti-Duplication (gates E3, E4, E7, R4).** ≥ 3 kill hypotheses in `plan.md` before searching; ≥ 30% of logged searches spent trying to prove them; contradicting evidence always confronted in the report body; never log the same quote with opposite stances.
4. **Primary Source Hierarchy & ≥ 50% T1–T3 Depth Floor (gates E6, E7).** Statutes, regulatory filings, binding contracts, and peer-reviewed papers (`T1`–`T3`) strictly override secondary press and encyclopedias (`T4`) and community forums (`T5`). Community/personal GitHub sources must be `T5`; tertiary encyclopedias/explainers (Wikipedia, Investopedia, Britannica, BabyPips) must be `T4` (never `T1`–`T3`). At least 50% of evidence in a study (≥ 4 items) must be `T1`–`T3`.
5. **Deterministic Epistemic Formula & Grounded EV Matrix (gate R10).** Compute the Epistemic Index ($\text{EI} \in [0, 100]$) and Mechanical Confidence Ceiling via `formula.py`. Every Decision & Sensitivity Matrix must be calibrated with deterministic `[C###]` calculations representing the **exact topic-specific economics** (holding period friction, holdout sample size $N$, expected $t$-stat, and deploy probability). **Never copy-paste generic €38.5k / 50-35-15 EV tables**, and never display positive net EV when the verdict is NONE FOUND or KILL. Declared overall confidence cannot exceed `formula.py`'s `max_confidence`.
6. **No self-scores, no unearned certainty (gate R6).** No "guaranteed", "proven", "certain", "10/10". Never fabricate URLs, quotes, IDs, or statistics.
7. **Calibrated Probabilities Only (gate R7).** No arbitrary probability intervals (`[0.02, 0.05, 0.12]`) or Bayesian priors (`π = 0.55`) without an explicit empirical sample size (`n=...`) or deterministic `[C###]` / `Calculation:` formula.
8. **Lab Data Dominance & Trap Registry (gate R8).** Internal empirical execution data strictly overrides published literature. All 8 registered trap patterns in `dr_engine/tools/known_traps.json` are mechanically enforced:
   - *London 4 PM Fix Reversal:* refuted in lab ($t = -11.09$, P31/FXFIX01/FXFIX02).
   - *Multi-Asset Seasonality:* monthly (not daily), gross Sharpe 0.32–0.37 decays out-of-sample, zero in FX, swap (>0.60 Sharpe) turns net returns negative, $N_{eff} \approx 4.23$ breadth. Never grant conditional approval without post-pub survival.
   - *Holding Costs vs Floors:* never compare annual swap rates to per-trade hurdle floors (G9); compute $C_{hold} = \text{spread} + \text{nights} \times \text{daily\_swap}$.
   - *CFD Long-Short Financing:* long pays interest, short receives interest; net spread is $r_{long} - r_{short} \approx 6.0\%$ p.a. (1.64 bp/day), not 850 bp flat. A 20% earnings gap in a 20-stock book is 1.0% equity loss, not 5%.
   - *FX Geometry vs Multi-Day:* EURUSD under 10 bp stop has $0.070R$ friction failing S2 ($>0.030R$); multi-day holding friction on indices/gold does not bind ($0.004–0.016R$).
   - *Legal analogies:* Natural person KYC does not confer EU consumer protection; commercial terms override.
   - *Community sources:* Unofficial wrappers/repos are T5, not T1/T2.
9. **External reports trigger Fact-Check.** Extract claims, fetch primary sources, verify verbatim quotes, recompute math via `calc.py`, flag hallucinations, and re-run `gate.py --live 5 --scorecard`.
10. **Scale, contradictions and entailment (v4.0, gates E4, R11, R12).** Above ~15 sources read ranked passages (`corpus.py build` / `triage` / `query --log`), never whole pages. Every search records what it found (on protocol 4.1 — `study.json` — kill searches without a result set do not count: E4 FAIL). State units fully (RT vs per side, p.a. vs per day) and convert with `units.py` / `calc.py`, never by eye. Load-bearing facts go into `claims.py`; every conflict it finds appears in the Conflicts section with evidence from both sides and its axis. Before delivery a fresh-context verifier judges the seeded `audit.py sample`; every error is fixed and re-judged; a known error blocks delivery whatever the bound.
11. **Discovery completeness & the runbook (v4.1 P2, gate E9).** Every discovery step records its result set (`discover.py --dir`, `--snowball`, `log-search --opened ... --channel`). On protocol 4.1 studies: `screening.py sample/record/bound` on the discarded leads (a lead judged relevant must be fetched: E9 FAIL) and `corpus.py stats --save` (stop at `SATURATED`, or explain the coverage verdict in Limitations). Follow the SKILL.md RUNBOOK in order and run `next_step.py research/<dir>` after every step; do exactly what it prints.
12. **Canonical storage & automated gate verification (Rule R21 — Anti-Bypass).** NEVER write raw `.md` summaries directly into `reports/`. Every v13 study must pass `lean_engine.py verify --dir research/<dir> --strict-v13` (enforcing R19 >= 1,000 prose words / >= 5 sources / >= 8 verbatim quotes / >= 2 tables, R20 `Claim -> URL -> Verbatim Quote` table, R17 IDI >= 100, R18 EAD, and B12 no hype verdicts / fake multipliers) and be exported via `lean_engine.py export --dir research/<dir> --out reports/<NAME>.md` (or `gate.py --live 5 --scorecard --require-audit` on Option B). Verify `reports/` cleanliness with `lean_engine.py audit-deliverables`.
13. **Autonomous Self-Improvement.** Persist every new trap pattern, primary registry, formula improvement, or tooling upgrade immediately into `SKILL.md`, `references/`, `known_traps.json`, and `tests/` without waiting for user prompts.
