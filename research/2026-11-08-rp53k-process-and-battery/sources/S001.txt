Title: From pixels to profits: trading arbitrage portfolios based on image representations
Authors: Niklas Paluszkiewicz | Year: 2026 | Citations: 0 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11408-026-00507-x

Abstract: Abstract This paper studies whether convolutional neural networks (CNNs) can extract economically useful signals from image representations of financial time series to form statistical arbitrage portfolios. Following a generalized pairs-trading framework, I encode the joint dynamics of each stock, its factor-based hedge, and the residual spread as RGB images. A CNN ranks stocks by the probability of above-median residual returns in the following month. The strongest baseline specification generates a gross monthly excess return of 0.82% and an annualized Sharpe ratio of 0.97. Hedging improves gross risk-adjusted performance, while comparisons with technical-indicator strategies suggest that conventional predictors capture part of the image-based performance. The image-based portfolio retains a positive alpha after controlling for these benchmarks. Overall, the findings suggest that CNNs can extract economically relevant patterns from image representations, although profitability depends on beta estimation and trading costs.
