Tofacilitateouranalysis,wegroupthefactorsintodifferentcategories.Westart

Strictly speaking, a risk factor should be a variable that has unpredictable
variation through time. Moreover, assets'risk exposures to this factor need

criteria, individual firm characteristics should not qualify as risk factors
