## 3. THE 6 NON-NEGOTIABLE FORENSIC QUANT RULES

Every research script, candidate model, and modification MUST comply. Failure on ANY rule equals immediate rejection (`FAIL ANYWHERE = FAIL`):

1. **Rule 1 — Realistic Bid/Ask Friction & Commission:** no mid-price illusions. Futures: MES $2.25/trade, MNQ $1.50/trade. **US100.cash: spread 1.45 pt + ~0.25 pt slippage ≈ 0.022R at a ~76-pt stop.** Spot FX ≥1.5–2.0 pips + commission. Gold ≥$0.50 spread + commission.
2. **Rule 2 — Zero Lookahead Bias:** signal on bar t−1 close; execution on bar t Open (`.shift(1)` / next open). Intrabar extremes cannot trigger entries before bar close.
3. **Rule 3 — Strict Session & Timezone Pinning:** `ZoneInfo("America/New_York")` for US RTH, `ZoneInfo("Europe/Prague")` for FTMO day boundaries, UTC for FX. FTMO MT5 server clock = New York + 7 h.
4. **Rule 4 — Canonical PnL:** Net = (exit_fill − entry_fill) × direction × multiplier − friction. Sign flipping is banned.
5. **Rule 5 — 2xCOST Survival (Acid Test):** t_2x > 1.0 with spreads and commissions doubled.
6. **Rule 6 — Chronological OOS Persistence:** t_OOS > 1.0, t_OOS / t_FIT > 0.5, win-rate drift < 10%, |ρ| < 0.20 vs Pillar 1. Multiple-testing bar: Bonferroni-adjusted t (≈2.5–2.6 for 4–10 tests), halves ≥ 1.0.
